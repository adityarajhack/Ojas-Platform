Guaranteed Welfare Delivery

How Documents Move Through The Verification Pipeline

Every application undergoes an immutable four-eyes verification cycle before public funds are disbursed.

1

Automated Rules & Cadastre Check

The rules engine validates age, caste, and income eligibility against the state cadastre. For household benefits like sanitary toilets, family rosters prevent duplicate disbursements.

2

Human Field Officer Scrutiny

If a household condition is flagged, the application is never auto-rejected. It routes automatically to the Block Development Officer for geo-tagged ground inspection and report submission.

3

Aadhaar Payment Bridge (DBT)

Once sanctioned, funds are pushed directly through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) into the citizen's Aadhaar-seeded bank account with zero middleman deductions.
