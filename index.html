National DPI Architecture

Departments stop working in silos with one unified record

Birth registration, identity, dwelling records, and welfare schemes have lived in separate digital silos for decades. Ojas joins them into one verified, continuous public infrastructure pipeline.

01
Citizen DPI

Lifelong citizen record

A single record from birth registration onwards — identity, income category, dependents, and documents kept live across ministries.

Scope: Individual Birth-to-Pension
02
Family Graph

Household & family linking

Every citizen is linked to their household dwelling unit and relations, so full family context is visible to verifying officers.

Scope: Dwelling Graph Connected
03
Anti-Fraud

Duplicate claim detection

Household-level schemes are cross-verified against prior allocations. Duplicates route to officers with context — never auto-rejected.

Accuracy: 99.8% Zero Auto-Rejection
04
Audit-Ready

Auditable trail

Every sanction, disbursement, and verification is logged with digital actor and timestamp — transparent for audit and vigilance.

Integrity: 100% Tamper-Proof
